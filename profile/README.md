# Curio

### A design library for AI agents.

Give your AI a visual language, not just a prompt. Curio turns design movements,
brand aesthetics, and cultural traditions into complete design systems —
with colors, typography, spacing, components, cultural context, and CSS techniques.

## Bring Curio to your AI

| Project | What you get | Get started |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **[Curio MCP](https://github.com/designbycurio/curio-mcp-extension)** | Search and browse the live library, preview styles, and retrieve design specs through OAuth. | [Connect your agent](https://github.com/designbycurio/curio-mcp-extension#install) |
| **[Curio Design Skill](https://github.com/designbycurio/curio-design-skill)** | 105 free design systems bundled with guidance for applying them to websites, slides, dashboards, posters, and more. | [Install the skill](https://github.com/designbycurio/curio-design-skill#install) |

The catalog contains **2,233 design systems**, including **105 free themes**
(as of September 18, 2026). The skill bundles the free collection; MCP connects
to the live catalog, with spec access based on your account and plan.

Each `DESIGN.md` combines structured design tokens with the context and
techniques that give a style its character. Choose a style, hand it to your
agent, and apply it to the work you already have.

> “Find a Japanese minimal style and apply it to my landing page.”
>
> “Use Curio Bauhaus to make a pitch deck.”
